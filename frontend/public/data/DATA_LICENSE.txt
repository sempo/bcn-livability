Data files of the time2map Barcelona livability map
====================================================

This folder holds files built by time2map from open data. The publishers, licences and dates of every source are
listed in the app ("Data sources" next to the map) and in docs/DATA_LICENSES.md of the repository.

Contact: Alexander Semenov, info@time2map.com

OpenStreetMap (ODbL 1.0)
------------------------
The following are derived from OpenStreetMap data, (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, and are made available under the
Open Database Licence 1.0 (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/):

  - ../barcelona_poi.pmtiles (points of interest extracted from OpenStreetMap)
  - the walking-minute columns min_* and the City Core columns cc_* of buildings-scores.bin.gz
  - area-start.json, which is computed from those columns

The derived data is available on request as plain tables (CSV): write to the contact above.

Other sources
-------------
The remaining columns and files keep the licence of their publisher and are NOT relicensed under the ODbL:

  - Dirección General del Catastro: buildings, addresses (CC BY 4.0, DG Catastro). Selected and combined by
    time2map; not official cadastral information.
  - Ajuntament de Barcelona (Open Data BCN): noise map, residents' survey, area boundaries (CC BY 4.0).
  - MITECO / SICA: noise maps outside Barcelona (CC BY 4.0).
  - Generalitat de Catalunya: flood zones (ACA), wildfire hazard, sale prices (INCASOL) under the
    "Llicencia oberta d'us d'informacio - Catalunya".
  - ICGC: base map style and municipality outlines (CC BY 4.0).
  - Street flooding (RESCCUE, Ajuntament de Barcelona / BCASA / Aquatec): no licence published. Shown for
    information only; no rights are granted to it here.

time2map is not affiliated with, and not endorsed by, any of these bodies. Where we transformed the data the app says
how.
